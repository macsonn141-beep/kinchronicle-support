KinChronicle Subscription Information

Pre-release product information · Last updated 8 October 2026.

Developer: Lian Chong Nui.

This page explains the first-release Free/Pro boundary and Apple-billed subscriptions. The purchase screen and Apple's confirmation show the exact product, localized price, duration, and any offer available to you before purchase.

Free access and Pro

Free includes up to 20 recorded events, all eight event categories, local Timeline, basic supporting-item attachments within stated local limits, record completeness, basic structured filters, basic PDF reports, basic CSV export, evidence manifests, and app lock. You can obtain useful record history before subscribing.

Pro adds unlimited recorded events, supported attachments with a 50 MiB per-file import limit, no subscription total-count cap, and actual device-capacity constraints, Pattern Intelligence, advanced structured search and filters, related-event chains, advanced report building and V1 export features, and optional AI assistance where supported. No promise of infinite device storage is made.

V1 U.S. launch configuration is US$12.99 monthly with no free trial, or US$89.99 annually with a seven-day free trial for eligible customers. No lifetime plan is offered. These are the locked launch decisions, not a substitute for actual StoreKit prices and eligibility. The first release is intended for the United States storefront. If storefront availability changes, the amount shown in Apple's purchase confirmation applies to the purchase you authorize.

Billing, renewal, and trial

Payment is handled by Apple through your Apple Account. Subscriptions continue to renew until canceled. Apple may charge for the next period during the 24 hours preceding it. An eligible annual trial becomes a paid annual subscription if it is not canceled. To avoid trial renewal, cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. See Apple Media Services Terms and Apple's cancellation instructions.

An annual trial offer shows its trial length and the annual price after the trial before purchase. The monthly plan has no free trial. Trial availability depends on Apple's eligibility rules and the offer shown for your Apple Account.

Cancellation, restore, and refunds

Manage an Apple-billed plan through Apple's subscription settings. Deleting the app, deleting records, or writing to support does not cancel billing. Use Restore Purchases for eligible purchases made with the same Apple Account; no new KinChronicle account is required. A refund request is handled under Apple's applicable process and consumer rights, without a promised result.

When Pro ends

Your existing local records, attachments, Timeline, and basic viewing, editing, search, and export remain available. Pro intelligence and advanced features may no longer be available, and additional event creation is limited by the Free allowance. Historical records do not disappear because a subscription expires. Restore Purchases cannot recover deleted or lost local family records.

Records and reports

You control the facts you enter and the materials you select. Import and share only material you are entitled to use, and check a report before sending it. The app permits corrections with revision history and deletion of your own private records; it does not claim an immutable or legally certified archive.

Required report notice:

This report summarizes records entered or imported by the user. It does not independently determine truth, legal significance, admissibility, or compliance.
