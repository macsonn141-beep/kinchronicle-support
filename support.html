KinChronicle Support

Pre-release product information · Last updated 8 October 2026.

KinChronicle is a private record organizer for adults documenting co-parenting events. It keeps expected and recorded actual information, supporting items, follow-up, outcomes, and source-traceable summaries together. It does not contact your co-parent or require them to join.

Contact

Email macsonn141@gmail.com with the subject KinChronicle Support. Include your app version, iOS/iPadOS version, device model, the action you attempted, what you expected, and the error shown. Use a short description that avoids identifying family information. If a screenshot is necessary, redact child names, addresses, message contents, school/medical information, and unrelated records. Never send passwords, payment-card details, or authentication codes.

A record is missing from the Timeline

Check the active date range, child, event type, status, evidence, completeness, and outcome filters, and clear filters to see the full local Timeline. Closed records remain part of your history. Check the original device: V1 does not synchronize records across devices. If a save or import error was shown, the action may not have completed. Do not remove the app as an initial troubleshooting step.

Completeness and factual calculations

Open Record Completeness to see which fields contribute to the score and which are missing. The score describes how much of the record is filled in. It does not verify the record or measure legal strength. Time differences and other metrics come from recorded fields; check the expected and actual dates, times, and time-zone context. Each pattern metric opens the records used to calculate it.

Attachments and exports

Select supporting items through Photos or Files. If import fails, check that the source file is available and supported, then retry after addressing the shown error. If an imported item is missing, support cannot recreate the original for you. Keep your source material where appropriate.

Reports summarize selected records and refer to supporting items. Review the selected date range and filters when a report has no records. Review the generated PDF or CSV before sharing it. A PDF/CSV export is not a guaranteed complete backup or a certified forensic archive. Keep any exports you need before deleting local records or removing the app.

Subscription access and purchases

Use Restore Purchases with the Apple Account used for the purchase. An internet connection may be needed to contact Apple. Restoration recovers eligible purchase access; it does not transfer the local records from another device. If products are unavailable, wait and retry rather than assuming a purchase succeeded. Existing local records remain accessible after Pro expires.

To manage or cancel an Apple-billed subscription, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions, select KinChronicle, and follow Apple's current instructions in Cancel a subscription from Apple. Removing the app or emailing support does not itself cancel an Apple-billed subscription.

Refund requests for Apple-billed purchases go through Apple's process; approval depends on eligibility. See Request a refund. No refund outcome is guaranteed by KinChronicle support.

AI and app lock

Optional on-device AI depends on device and system-model availability. If unavailable, continue with manual recording and the core features. An AI suggestion is not a finding about another person. Review suggestions before approving them.

If app lock authentication fails, follow the available system authentication options. Do not disclose your device passcode to support. The developer cannot remotely unlock your device or recover records from a developer cloud database.

Important boundaries

KinChronicle organizes user-entered and imported records. It does not provide legal advice, medical advice, custody recommendations, truth determination, or guaranteed admissibility. It is not an emergency communication service. For professional interpretation of your records, consult an appropriate qualified professional.
