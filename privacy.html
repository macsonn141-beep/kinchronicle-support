KinChronicle Privacy Policy

Developer: Lian Chong Nui.

Last updated: 8 October 2026.

KinChronicle is in pre-release preparation. This policy describes the first-release privacy design and the current support website. App-specific behavior will be checked against the shipping release before availability.

KinChronicle helps adults organize factual co-parenting event records, supporting items, follow-up, outcomes, patterns, and reports. You can use its core record features without creating a KinChronicle account or involving your co-parent.

Records you choose to keep

You decide what to enter and import. Records may include event dates, expected and recorded actual details, child nicknames, locations you type, expense information, communication topics, school or care information, factual notes, follow-up, and outcomes. You may attach photos, screenshots, receipts, PDFs, or documents you select.

The app also creates local identifiers, creation/import timestamps, revision entries, and calculations such as time differences and record completeness. These organize your records; they do not independently verify what happened.

Enter only information needed for your own documentation. Children do not create accounts or use the app independently. A child profile does not require an address, school, medical history, or identity document.

Local storage and core processing

Core records and imported copies are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Search, completeness, factual pattern calculations, and report preparation use those local records. KinChronicle does not provide a developer-operated account database or cloud synchronization for V1, and does not automatically upload your family records to the developer.

The first-release design uses iOS/iPadOS data protection for its database and imported attachments. An optional app lock uses the system's authentication features on supported devices. These protections depend on your device and its settings; they are not a guarantee against every form of access or data loss. The developer does not receive your Face ID or Touch ID biometric templates through the app lock.

Device backups are separate from app synchronization

Local-first storage does not mean that device data can never be copied elsewhere. Apple's device backup services can include app data, depending on device settings and which app files are eligible for backup. A backup is different from live cloud synchronization between installations. Apple's explanation of app data in backups is available in What does iCloud back up?.

The first-release design keeps persistent records and imported attachments eligible for ordinary Apple device backups according to your settings, and excludes temporary export files. Release verification is pending while this product is in pre-release preparation.

Importing and sharing

KinChronicle imports only items you choose using system Photos or Files pickers. It does not harvest your photo library, intercept messages, access another person's account, covertly record conversations, or track children or co-parents in the background. Location text is manually entered in the first-release design.

When you export or share a report, CSV, or evidence manifest, you choose its destination in the system share interface. The destination app, storage provider, or recipient may receive and keep that material under its own practices. Sharing or saving an export creates a separate copy; deleting a KinChronicle record does not erase copies already shared or stored elsewhere. Temporary export copies inside KinChronicle are intended to be cleaned after the share lifecycle where feasible; exported copies you choose to keep are under your control.

Optional on-device AI

Where available, optional AI assistance processes the records or material you choose using Apple's on-device system model. It can suggest neutral wording or supported factual organization. You choose whether to approve a suggestion. Suggestions do not silently replace your factual record and do not determine truth or legal significance.

The first-release design has no third-party cloud AI provider and no automatic external-provider fallback. If the on-device model is unavailable, core recording, search, calculations, and reports remain available. AI assistance is shown as unavailable. The app identifies which optional AI assistance is available on your device.

Purchases and Apple services

Apple handles App Store payments. The app uses StoreKit transaction and subscription-status information to determine access to Pro features; the developer does not receive your complete payment-card details through StoreKit. Apple may process purchase, subscription, and diagnostic information under its own policies, and provides developers with purchase/subscription reports. See App Store & Privacy.

The first-release design includes no advertising SDK, developer analytics SDK, behavioral advertising, sale of family data, or developer telemetry containing record contents. Device-level diagnostic choices and Apple's service practices are separate from developer-added analytics. The final privacy disclosure and App Store privacy answers will be checked against the actual app and SDKs.

Support messages

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and whatever you choose to include. Email providers process those messages under their own policies. Support information is used to answer the request, diagnose the issue, and maintain necessary correspondence. Do not send private family records, child identifiers, account passwords, payment details, or unredacted evidence unless you deliberately choose to share material needed for a specific support issue.

Support correspondence is kept only as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations. Contact the developer to ask about access, correction, or deletion of information you sent to support. Any retention needed for an unresolved issue or legal obligation will be explained where applicable. The developer cannot remotely retrieve or delete records that remain solely inside your device's app storage.

Your local data controls

You can edit or delete your private records in the app and export the records supported by your plan. Deleting an event removes it from your local Timeline and future reports; related imported copies are cleaned according to the app's deletion process. It does not delete the original item from Photos, Messages, Mail, Files, or another app. It also does not erase a past device backup, an exported report, or a copy held by someone else.

Your existing records and attachments remain accessible when Pro expires. You retain the basic viewing, editing, search, and export functions described in the app. A subscription does not create a developer-held recovery copy. Device loss, removing the app, or deleting local data can cause record loss; a purchase restore restores eligible subscription access, not your family record history.

Policy changes and contact

The published policy will identify its revision date. Any future cloud service, external AI provider, analytics, or changed data transfer requires an updated disclosure before that behavior is offered.
